# Approvals

Changes from people who are not admins wait for a reviewer, so no one can quietly change what a check does.

## What needs approval

| Change | Admin | Everyone else |
|---|---|---|
| Create a check | Applied at once | Needs approval |
| Edit your own check | Applied at once | Applied at once |
| Edit someone else's check | Applied at once | Needs approval |
| Delete a check | Applied at once | Needs approval |

Managers and admins can approve or reject.

## Reviewing

Open **Approvals** in the Dock. **Pending Approvals** lists each request with the requester, the time, the check and a note. Choose **Approve** or **Reject** and add a comment. **Approval History** keeps every decision.
