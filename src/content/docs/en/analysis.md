# Analysis

**Analysis** in the sidebar shows how your checks have run over time.

## Filters

- **Time range** — the last 7, 30 or 90 days, or all time. Days are counted in your own time zone.
- **Check** — one check or all of them.
- **Tags** — only checks with the chosen tags.

## What you see

- **Totals** — how many runs there were, and how many came back **Broken**, **Issues** or **Clean**, each with its share of all runs.
- **Outcomes** — the same split as a chart.
- **Runs per day** — every day of the range, all runs and each outcome, to spot a check that started failing.
- **By day** — the most recent days (up to 14), newest first.
- **By check** — each check's runs per outcome, its clean rate (the share of runs that found nothing) and when it last ran.
